Dwg No. CS‑02 · Leadership

Led by a Board Certified
Quality & Regulatory Specialist.

Taiyon Davis is the Founder, CEO, and Chief Consultant of Cardinal Solutions. A Board Certified Pharmacy Technician, he has personally designed and built two licensed compounding pharmacies from the ground up — both fully operational today — and has led regulatory, quality, and operations functions across hospital, retail, and independent compounding pharmacy settings.

Cardinal Solutions exists to give pharmacies and pharmaceutical organizations the frameworks, documentation, and hands-on training they need to minimize regulatory risk and maximize operational performance. Formulation science support is available for select engagements, developed alongside licensed pharmacist oversight.
